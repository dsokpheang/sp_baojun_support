Your apps, on your Baojun.

Tools for Baojun head units running LingOS — open the apps you install, keep them inside the screen, and reach the Android settings the car hides.

Software

SP Baojun Kit — on the car

ADB on/off, a shortcut into engineering mode with the day's code, every hidden Android settings page, and automatic updates for itself and the launcher.

应用商店 · SP Baojun launcher — on the car

An app drawer the stock home screen can open. Tap any app to open it in a window that fits between the car's bars; long-press for app info; Clear all closes background apps.

SP Store — on the car

Search for an app, verify it and install it — plus install an APK already on the car, and get PSP games for PPSSPP. Install method is picked automatically.

Releases

SP Baojun Kit updates itself and the launcher from the release folder. Each file is checked against a published size and SHA‑256 before it is installed, so a partial or substituted download is refused.

Help

The support page covers installing, the home screen forgetting apps after a restart, and apps hiding under the top bar.
