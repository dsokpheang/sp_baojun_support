Privacy

SP Baojun collects nothing about you or your car.

What leaves the car

Only update checks: SP Baojun Kit downloads index.json and, when there is a newer version, the APK files from this site (GitHub Pages / raw.githubusercontent.com). No account, no identifier, no location, no vehicle data is sent. GitHub may log the request as any web server does.

What stays on the car

The Kit's own settings (auto-update on/off, last check time). The launcher stores nothing.
